## [1.0.25] - 2026-11-28

### Changed
- Dashboard corner controls are 25% smaller (text, icons and padding). Date and currency controls stay together on one line, scaling down together when necessary.
- Floating snackbars throughout the app are replaced by inline feedback that does not overlay content; error messages and Undo remain available.
- Choose color uses the selected account color with a contrasting label. The account icon opens the same color picker.
- Planned payment cards and details show title/amount, then category hierarchy/due date on one line, then schedule/account. Account names use the light accent color.
- Removed redundant day-count urgency badges while retaining the red left-edge accent.

### Fixed
- Long categories no longer push scheduled-payment metadata out of order.
- Update messages no longer promise an entirely custom/background update. Google Play's consent and installation screens remain required.

## [1.0.24] - 2026-11-27

### Changed
- Home widget currency and date-range dropdowns are taller, with larger text and icons; on compact phones the date pill moves below the title.
- Tag breakdown shows the net amount per tag (income minus expenses), signed and coloured.
- All accounts tiles are shorter, and single-currency balances sit centred in the space under the name.
- Active History filter buttons are shown in the accent colour.
- Hide from All accounts in account details is now a switch matching the other toggles.

### Fixed
- Planned payment cards no longer truncate titles while space is free, keep "Skipped" labels on one line beside their icon, and place the due-soon badge on the status line instead of an extra row.
- Loan details and loan cards showed "Paid: 0" despite posted repayments; paid now sums actual repayments.

## [1.0.23] - 2026-11-27

### Changed
- Credit utilization now opens with a total credit summary: the combined percentage used across all credit cards with a limit, a progress bar, the amount used out of the total limit and the credit still available. The per-card list underneath has even spacing.
- The Account groups widget was redesigned: each group shows its account-type icon, the number of accounts and a bar comparing its size with the largest group. The header counts all accounts and groups, and rows are evenly spaced without dividers. Savings, investment and loan accounts get their own icons.
- The account deletion screen was redesigned with a status header, a step checklist (cloud data, sign-in credentials, this device) showing waiting, in-progress, done or failed for each step, a separate privacy note and a full-width action button.

## [1.0.22] - 2026-11-27

### Added
- Google Play updates now use a custom 1Wallet update sheet with a live download progress bar (percentage and MB downloaded). After you tap Update now and confirm Google Play's one-time prompt, the update downloads in the background, then installs and restarts the app automatically, with no Later step. The sheet only appears after the app is unlocked.

### Changed
- The automatic Google Play update check runs at most once per calendar day when the app opens or returns to the foreground. Several opens on the same day, or returning after days away, trigger only one check. Failed checks retry after 3 hours, sideloaded builds stop checking for the day, and an update that already downloaded installs instead of downloading again.
- Planned payment cards show the schedule and account on one line (for example "Every 2 months on 17 from HDFC Savings", or "into" for income), spanning the card width. The plan's notes appear underneath across the full card and wrap onto more lines only when needed; notes that just repeat the plan name or were written by the app are hidden.

### Fixed
- Tapping Skip next on a planned payment more than once no longer records the same skipped occurrence twice.
- Loan repayment history no longer lists skipped EMIs or counts them as posted repayments.
- One-time planned payments read "One-time" instead of "Every 1 once".

### Added
- Pulling down to refresh the Review inbox now also scans the last day of SMS and the active notifications from monitored apps, adding any new transactions. It runs silently, only for capture sources that are switched on and already permitted, and never brings back items that were already confirmed or dismissed.

## [1.0.20] - 2026-11-27

- The "Tour progress is saved on this device. Cloud sync is unavailable." message no longer pops up repeatedly. Tour progress is saved on the device first and its background cloud sync is now silent: it skips the cloud when nothing has changed (instead of a server transaction on every tour step, hint and app resume) and allows 20 seconds instead of 5 for a reconnecting network.
